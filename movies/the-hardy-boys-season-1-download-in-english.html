The Premise: A Summer of Secrets in Bridgeport

The narrative of The Hardy Boys 2020 Season 1 kicks off with a heartbreaking catalyst. We are introduced to sixteen-year-old Frank Hardy (Rohan Campbell) and his twelve-year-old brother, Joe Hardy (Alexander Elliot). The brothers are leading a relatively normal life in the city until their mother, Laura Hardy, is killed in a tragic accident. Reeling from the loss, their father, seasoned private investigator Fenton Hardy (played by James Tupper), decides to relocate the boys for the summer to the idyllic, quiet coastal town of Bridgeport. They are sent to live with their Aunt Trudy (Bea Santos), expecting nothing more than a dull, grieving summer.

However, Bridgeport is far from the sleepy town it appears to be on the surface. Fenton Hardy’s sudden departure on a secret, undercover investigation raises red flags for the boys. Convinced that their mother’s death was not a mere accident but part of a larger conspiracy tied to Bridgeport, Frank and Joe take it upon themselves to uncover the truth. What begins as a simple search for answers quickly spirals into a dangerous cat-and-mouse game involving secret societies, powerful local families, and a mysterious, radioactive artifact known as "The Eye".

Book vs. TV Show Adaptation: What Changed?

For longtime fans of the books, the 2020 television adaptation offers plenty of easter eggs while forging its own path. The original novels were episodic, with the brothers solving a new, standalone case in each book. The Hulu series takes a serialized approach, dedicating the entire 13-episode arc of Season 1 to a single, massive conspiracy. The stakes are much higher and deeply personal. Furthermore, characters like Biff Hooper, who was traditionally a male character in the books, have been reimagined (played brilliantly by female actress Riley O'Donnell), adding fresh dynamics to the core group of friends.
