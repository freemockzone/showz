Operating fighter jets like the MiG-21, MiG-23, MiG-27, and Mirage 2000 in thin high-altitude air severely reduced engine thrust and weapon payload capacities. Furthermore, the jagged terrain offered virtually no margin for error, exposing pilots to surface-to-air missiles and anti-aircraft fire while diving into narrow Himalayan gorges. Season 1 portrays these harrowing operational realities with meticulous attention to detail, making it an essential watch for enthusiasts of military aviation and historical strategy.
