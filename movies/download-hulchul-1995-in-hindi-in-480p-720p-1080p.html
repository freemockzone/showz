Hulchul (1995)

★ 6.5 1995 2h 48min Hindi Only Action / Thriller / Drama
Hulchul is a compelling 1995 Bollywood action-thriller directed by Anees Bazmee in his directorial debut. The film is historic for being the very first cinematic pairing of real-life couple Ajay Devgn and Kajol, whose electric on-screen chemistry added immense depth to the narrative. The plot revolves around Devva, a young man who is wrongfully convicted of a murder he did not commit and imprisoned. Stripped of his freedom and reputation, he escapes from custody to track down the actual conspirators, unraveling a deep web of deceit, political corruption, and betrayal. Supported by stellar performances from veteran powerhouses like Amrish Puri and Vinod Khanna, Hulchul remains a notable milestone of 90s action cinema.

Comprehensive Overview of Anees Bazmee's Directorial Debut

Released in April 1995, Hulchul marked a significant turning point in 90s commercial cinema by blending high-stakes investigative elements with gritty action set pieces and intense dramatic conflict. Directed by Anees Bazmee—who had previously established himself as a prominent dialogue and screenplay writer—the project showcased his ability to handle complex thriller narratives. Beyond its gripping storyline, the film achieved legendary status among cinephiles for uniting Ajay Devgn and Kajol on screen for the very first time, paving the way for one of Bollywood's most celebrated romantic pairings.


The thematic core of the film explores the harrowing ordeal of miscarriage of justice and institutional corruption. The protagonist, Devva, represents resilience against systemic oppression, fighting not just for his personal freedom, but to expose the powerful syndicate that manipulated the legal framework to frame an innocent citizen. Supported by high-octane action choreography and dramatic courtroom confrontations, the film kept audiences glued to their seats from start to finish.

In-Depth Plot Summary and Narrative Progression

The cinematic narrative opens with an atmosphere of suspense, introducing Devva (Ajay Devgn), a simple, hardworking young man whose life is upended by a sudden tragedy. A prominent figure is murdered under mysterious circumstances, and due to fabricated evidence and compromised law enforcement officials, Devva is arrested, tried, and sentenced to a harsh prison term. Despite his vehement protests of innocence, the judicial system fails him, leaving him locked behind bars with a burning desire for truth.


Inside prison, Devva refuses to accept his fate. Recognizing that the real killers are roaming free and protecting their syndicate, he orchestrates a daring escape. Once on the outside, he becomes a fugitive hunted by the police while simultaneously hunting down the actual culprits. During his underground crusade, he crosses paths with Sharmili (Kajol), who initially gets caught in his path but soon realizes the truth behind his mission, offering her unwavering support and love.


As Devva draws closer to unmasking the criminal mastermind Shobharaj (Amrish Puri), the stakes escalate exponentially. Allies are tested, traps are laid, and dangerous confrontations erupt across urban hideouts. The climax features an intense showdown where Devva exposes the corruption, clears his name, and restores justice, cementing the victory of truth over tyranny.


Key Thematic Elements & Cinematic Highlights

To understand why Hulchul holds a special place in 90s action cinema retrospectives, consider the following defining elements:

  • Historic On-Screen Pairing: The film marks the debut collaboration between Ajay Devgn and Kajol, laying the foundation for their iconic real and reel-life chemistry.
  • Anees Bazmee's Directional Launch: Showcased Bazmee's versatility long before his dominance in slapstick comedy, proving his mastery over serious action thrillers.
  • Intense Antagonistic Dynamics: Amrish Puri delivered another chilling performance as a menacing villain, matched by Ajay Devgn's trademark silent intensity.
  • Gritty Action Choreography: Raw, earth-bound action sequences typical of mid-90s Bollywood blockbusters anchor the thriller pacing.

Production History & Behind-The-Scenes Trivia

The production of Hulchul remains historic because it brought together Ajay Devgn and Kajol during the early stages of their respective cinematic careers, well before their marriage in 1999. Anees Bazmee transitioned successfully into full-scale direction with this film, earning widespread appreciation from critics for maintaining a tight narrative grip without letting the momentum dip.
